FLYTBASE BDR HACKATHON / THOUGHT PROCESS

Evidence before outreach.

This workflow turns a campaign brief into an inspectable account and outreach queue. Each stage has a verification gate, so unknown facts do not become fabricated prospect data.

INPUT / ICP MODEL

Read the brief

Use the target vertical, SQM reference account, call-booking goal, and FlytBase angle to define the ICP.

STAGE 1

Account identification

Find LATAM miners, then score commodity, footprint, operational complexity, and public evidence against SQM.

STAGE 2

Contact discovery

Find Operations, HSE, and Site leaders. Retain a named contact only when a company-owned source verifies their role.

STAGE 3

Account research

Synthesize recent news, operating footprint, and technology or safety signals into a cited account brief.

STAGE 4

Personalized email

Use the verified remit and research evidence to generate outreach, then expose its inputs in the output queue.

Decision rules

  • First-party public evidence is the minimum standard for a factual claim.
  • Account fit is separate from contact verification.
  • Publicly unverified email addresses are never inferred.
  • Every email must name the exact evidence that shaped it.

Safe failure path

When account research succeeds but no target-role contact can be verified, retain the account and suppress the persona and email. Flag it for compliant enrichment or deeper first-party verification, then rerun.
